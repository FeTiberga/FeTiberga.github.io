Selected Work

Greedy Algorithms for Assortment Optimization

Spring 2023

Independent research at Cornell Tech investigating assortment optimization in e-retail under the Multinomial Logit (MNL) and Mixtures of MNL choice models. The work evaluates the practical performance of greedy algorithms — prized for their speed and simplicity — against exact integer-programming solutions, across a range of price and attraction-value distributions. A probabilistic analysis complements the computational study to characterize when greedy approaches recover near-optimal revenue.

Python MNL & Mixtures of MNL Integer Programming Probabilistic Analysis

A Machine Learning Model to Predict Tennis Matches

2022 – present

A long-running personal project predicting ATP and WTA match outcomes. At its core is a custom variation of the Glicko-2 rating system, fed into an LSTM that captures player form, fatigue, head-to-head history, and performance against opponents with similar playstyle. Built on a curated pipeline ingesting 400k+ historical matches, with extensive feature elimination, hyperparameter tuning, and uncertainty quantification. Achieves ~70% prediction accuracy and an average 7% ROI per match on the held-out test set.

Python PyTorch (LSTM) MySQL Glicko-2
