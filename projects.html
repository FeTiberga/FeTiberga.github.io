Selected Work

Assortment Optimization under Substitution

2025

Built a simulation and optimization pipeline for a retail assortment problem where customers substitute across products when their first choice is unavailable. Applied mixed-integer programming and choice-model estimation (MNL, nested logit) to benchmark against heuristic shelf-stocking policies. Showed meaningful revenue uplift versus naive approaches on synthetic and semi-real data.

Python Gurobi Discrete Choice Models Simulation

Tennis Serve Analytics

2024

A personal exploration of ATP serve data — combining publicly available match statistics with shot-quality metrics to model first-serve effectiveness as a function of court surface, opponent rank, and match state. Uses gradient-boosted trees and SHAP values to interpret which factors drive aces versus forced errors on different surfaces.
