Federico Tiberga

PhD Student · Management Science & Operations · London Business School

I'm a doctoral student at London Business School, where I study the intersection of machine learning, financial markets, and decision-making under uncertainty. My work focuses on how algorithmic strategies behave when rigorously tested — and why so many fail when they reach the real world.

Before my PhD I worked in finance and consulting across Europe. Outside the office I follow tennis closely — from Grand Slam statistics to serve mechanics — and find that good architecture and good research share the same discipline: constraint breeds clarity.

Research Interests
Machine Learning in Finance
Evaluating the real predictive power of ML-based asset pricing strategies and the methodological pitfalls that inflate backtested performance.
Empirical Asset Pricing
Cross-sectional return predictability, factor models, and the challenge of distinguishing genuine risk premia from data artifacts.
Decision-Making Under Uncertainty
How firms and investors make choices when outcomes are ambiguous, and how operations research methods can improve those decisions.
Algorithmic Strategy Evaluation
Developing rigorous frameworks for testing quantitative strategies so that in-sample success is not mistaken for out-of-sample alpha.
